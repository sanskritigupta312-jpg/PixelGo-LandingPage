The Problem We Solve

The Fragmentation
Trap.

Indian hospitality businesses manage 4–5 separate tools — Excel for bookings, WhatsApp for orders, a standalone POS, a separate accounting system. The result is 25% of profit quietly disappearing between the cracks.

₹ Real money is disappearing every month. Restaurant orders not charged to rooms. Bar bills forgotten. PixelGo closes every one of these gaps.
💸

Revenue Leakage Between Departments

Money disappears between restaurant POS and front desk. A guest orders food, it never gets added to their room bill. Multiplied across 30 guests — it's significant daily loss.

🕳️

Zero Real-Time Visibility

Owners ask "where am I losing money?" and get no answer. Legacy tools show total sales — not real profit, not waste, not pilferage. Every decision is a guess.

👤

Dangerous Staff Dependency

Operations collapse when a key staff member leaves. The process lives in people's heads — not in systems. PixelGo embeds the process in software, not in individuals.
